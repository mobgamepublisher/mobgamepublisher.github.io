Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile games published by mobgamepublisher ("we", "us") collect, how it is used and what choices you have. It applies to all of our games and to this website. If you have any questions, write to mobgamepublisher@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or exact location. The following information is collected automatically while you play:

  • Game data: your progress, score, level, settings, purchases (as "owned" flags) and a record of how you use the game (for example, how long a round lasts). Most of this is stored only on your device.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash and performance reports, and your IP address (from which an approximate region can be derived).
  • Advertising identifier: the advertising ID of your device (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) when you allow it, used to show and measure ads.
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, whether they were watched or clicked, and estimated revenue per ad.

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and keep it stable (fixing crashes and bugs).
  • To understand how the game is played and to improve its balance and features.
  • To show ads, including rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and to measure how well they perform.
  • To adjust some game settings for groups of players (for example, how often an offer appears), using remote configuration.
  • To protect the game against fraud and abuse and to meet legal obligations.

3. Advertising

Our games are supported by advertising. We use AppLovin MAX to run ad auctions between several ad networks, including Google AdMob and others. These partners may collect and use the data described above (such as your advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions) to deliver, personalise and measure ads, and may combine it with data from other apps they serve. Each partner acts under its own privacy policy:

  • AppLovin privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings: on Android, Settings > Privacy > Ads (delete or reset your advertising ID); on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising. Ads will still appear, but they will be less relevant to you.

Some of our games offer an option to remove banner and full-screen ads with an in-app purchase. Optional rewarded ads stay available so that you can choose to watch them for in-game rewards.

4. Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised advertising or non-essential data collection starts, using a consent screen. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings. If you are in a US state with a privacy law that gives you the right to opt out of the sale, sharing or targeted advertising use of your data, you can use the same settings or your device's advertising controls, or write to us.

5. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase (including Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to count how the game is used, to find crashes and to deliver configuration values. Firebase processes data under its own privacy terms.

6. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or under the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it. Parents can also use the family controls of their device or app store.

7. Sharing information

We share information only with the service providers described above (advertising, analytics, crash reporting, app store billing) so that they can perform their services, and where required by law. We do not sell your information for money.

8. Retention

Game data lives on your device until you delete the app. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period set by our providers (typically up to 14 months), after which it is deleted or aggregated.

9. International transfers

Our service providers process data in several countries, including outside your own. Where required, they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not keep a name or account for you, we may need your advertising ID or device details to find the data. Write to mobgamepublisher@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will say so in the game or on this page.
